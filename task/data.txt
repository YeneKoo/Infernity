SUMMARY

Infernity is a fire-safety dashboard for NASA's microgravity combustion research. We curated 16 NASA PSI investigations into a typed, searchable catalog covering fuels, oxygen and pressure conditions, hardware, sensors, findings, and fire-safety relevance. Users can filter instantly, compare up to three experiments, explore oxygen and hardware trends, and walk through a 7-slide pitch deck. For a specific habitat scenario, an AI model ranks the catalog and explains each match with stated assumptions; AI-generated summaries stream in Markdown for researcher or student audiences. Users bring their own OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and credentials stay in browser storage. The project addresses the challenge by making a large public record easier to find, compare, and interpret for spacecraft, lunar bases, and rovers. It matters because future habitats may run at higher oxygen concentrations, and better access to flammability and suppression evidence supports safer material and fire-safety decisions.
